# Follow-up email

1. Find the meeting in memory: attendees, decisions, open items, promised materials.
2. Draft a reply in the owner's style: short paragraphs, no filler.
3. Recap decisions in at most three bullets.
4. End with exactly one clear ask and a proposed date.
5. Save it as a draft for the owner. Never send it.

## Output
Subject line and body, under 150 words.

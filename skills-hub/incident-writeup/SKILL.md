# Incident write-up

1. Build the timeline from the activity log and messages: detection, response, resolution.
2. State the impact in plain terms: who was affected, for how long.
3. Describe the cause as a chain of contributing factors, not a person.
4. List what went well and what slowed the response.
5. Propose follow-ups, each as an issue with an owner and a date.

## Output
Headings: Summary, Timeline, Impact, Cause, What went well, Follow-ups. Blameless wording throughout.

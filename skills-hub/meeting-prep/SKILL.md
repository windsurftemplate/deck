# Meeting prep

1. Search memory for the person and their company: past calls, decisions, open issues.
2. If research is available, look up recent company news from professional sources only.
3. Never include personal-life details (home, family, health, personal accounts).
4. Write three talking points tied to what the owner wants from the meeting.
5. Write two open questions that move the relationship forward.

## Output
Headings: Who, Company context, Our history, Talking points, Questions, Watch-outs. Under 300 words.

# 4. The deck and Crew chat

## The 3D deck

The Deck page shows your crew as astronauts on a space station sitting on an asteroid. It is a live picture of what the crew is doing, not decoration.

### Stations

| Station | Who | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Command | Chief of Staff | Today's work and approvals on the big screen |
| Comms | GTM | Drafts waiting for review |
| Engineering | Engineering | Open issues |
| Operations | Operations | Tracker work |
| Science lab | Research | Research tasks |
| Archive | Memory | Facts, documents and skills; lights up when the crew learns |
| Reactor core | Models | Tokens used today against your budget; glows while the crew works, dims when stopped |
| Vault | CISO | Everything waiting for you, each with the CISO's risk opinion; sends a beam when you approve |

### Status rings and labels

| Ring | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Blue | Working on a task (and typing at a glowing console) |
| Amber, pulsing | Needs you: an approval is waiting |
| Red, flashing | Last task not finished |
| Grey | Standby |

When a crew member takes a task, it walks to Command and back. When it reports, it walks there again. Idle crew take short walks around their rooms.

### Using it

- Drag to turn the view, scroll or pinch to zoom.
- Click a station, or use the buttons along the bottom, to fly there. A panel explains the station and lists anything waiting, with **Approve** and **Reject**.
- **Back to overview** returns to the full view.
- The Archive panel has **Open the second brain**.

All motion stops if your system has reduced motion turned on.

## List view

The List view shows the same information without 3D: crew status on the left, chat on the right. Use it on older machines or when you want less on screen.

## Crew chat

Crew chat is where you watch the crew work and where crew members talk to each other.

### Live activity

Every step of delegated work appears in order:

| Label | Example |
|---|---|
| Hands off | Chief of Staff gives Engineering a task with its done-when list |
| Uses a tool | Engineering creates an issue (with the result) |
| Reports | Engineering reports back to the Chief of Staff |
| Checks | The verifier confirms the work or lists what is missing |
| Asks you | GTM asks to send an email |
| Decides | You approved or rejected it |

Use **Show** to filter by one agent.

### Discussions

Start a discussion when you want the crew's views before deciding something.

1. Write a topic, such as "Should we build SSO before the Acme pilot?"
2. Choose who joins and how many rounds (1 to 3).
3. **Start discussion**. Each crew member speaks in turn, sees what the others said, and can agree, disagree or add a risk.
4. The Chief of Staff sums up: where the crew agrees, where it does not, and up to three next steps.

You can add your own message while it runs; the next speakers see it. Discussions are talk only: no tools run and nothing is sent anywhere. Each discussion is saved and logged to memory.
